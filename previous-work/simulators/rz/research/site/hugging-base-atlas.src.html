Hugging BaseBase Power × AITX hackathonAustin · 26 Sep 2026

ERCOT sees one number.The street sees 379 transformers.

ERCOT dispatches Base's fleet as one number per load zone and does not enforce neighbourhood limits. Hugging Base simulates the feeder underneath that number, so Base can see what each dispatch, each new battery and each failure does on the street.

Where every number comes from

Python computes, files sit in between, and the browser only plays what was computed. Click a box to trace what feeds it and what it feeds.

Built (on main) Next (agreed in reconciliation) Stretch

Replay is the spine

Nothing on stage computes live. Every page plays files the simulator wrote ahead of time, so the demo cannot crash on a slow solve or a dropped connection. Select a box to see its role, its format and where it lives in the repo.

Three rules every box follows

  • OpenDSS decides every violation. The controller's kW estimate never judges itself.
  • No language model produces a setpoint, base point or rank. Models only draft scenarios and explain numbers.
  • The 20% member reserve is a hard limit in every strategy, never traded for market position.

      When pieces fail

      The track scores how the system holds up when parts of it break. Here the part that breaks is our own controller. Step through it.

      Hugging Base/stories/pieces-failRECORDED RUNTIME · PLANNED
      1. 1
        Every command is fencedEach carries a controller epoch, a sequence number and a 30-second expiry. The battery enforces them, so a dead or confused controller can do no harm.
      2. 2
        Leases, not luckWorkers hold 3-second leases on groups of transformers. When one dies, the coordinator hands its group to another at a higher epoch.
      3. 3
        Silence is safeA battery that stops hearing from the cloud goes stale after 180 s and idles with backup armed when its command expires. A neighbour on the same feeder covers.

      What ERCOT's own files show

      Two findings from public ERCOT data that most readers miss. These charts are real, pulled without a key.

      ERCOT's record-demand day, 22 Jul 2026: 15-minute real-time prices

      Three load zones move together. The evening spike peaks at $344/MWh at 22:00 in LZ_NORTH; midday energy is cheap.

      Source: ERCOT NP6-785-ER real-time settlement point prices. Interval start, Central time.

      When is the cheapest 2 hours to recharge?

      Start hour of each summer day's cheapest 2-hour block, Jun–Aug 2026.

      Perfect foresight, zone level. Worth about $0.40 per Core per day against the naive post-peak recharge.

      The Texas grid got stiffer per MW lost

      Frequency dip per MW of generation lost, every event since 2015.

      ERCOT NP12-261-M. The reporting standard changed in 2022 and recent years have few events, so read the trend with care. A 1,000-battery hijack moves frequency about 3–17 mHz: inside normal wander.

      Why Base caresArbitrage revenue is shrinking. Knowing the recharge window sits on the morning solar ramp, not right after the evening peak, is worth money and also takes stress off feeders at night.
