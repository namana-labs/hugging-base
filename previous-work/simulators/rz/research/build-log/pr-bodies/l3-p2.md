### What it does
- `sim/siting.py`: the stateless 5.4.3 core `per_tf_rule()`, vectorised and tested against its literal sequential walk (600 random states) and against L2's `allocate(state=None, cover=False)`. The month engine runs 3,000 x 15 min per "world" (a transformer plus its batteries; transformers are independent in the surrogate, so "one more battery on tf i" for every i is one run). The zone signal is per battery (5.4.2's discharge plan per day from its own energy; `d26` all at once from `onset_d26`, or `cheapest`, until 06:00). Aware is capped by its own transformer only; naive is uncapped. Month metrics: h100, normal-tier events, emergency intervals, protection at 15-min steps, battery-caused attribution.
- `sim/p2_build.py`: 16 combos (policy x class x rule x growth). The rank rule has no weights: no new violation, stress hours avoided, `peakWithPct`, revenue minus curtailment cost, id. - `sim/referee.py`: 6 OpenDSS month runs (baseline, top-5 greedy build and +20%, both policies), error and tier agreement on the shortlist, and OpenDSS `before`/`after` on both default combos' top-5 cards. It is keyed by the schedules' sha256, so a stale referee file is never merged.
