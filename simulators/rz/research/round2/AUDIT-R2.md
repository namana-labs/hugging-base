- **Auditor:** the round-2 data auditor. This audit is read-only: no repo edits, no commits.
- **When:** 26 Sep 2026, about 14:30–15:30 UTC.

## Verdict

**The P1 physics is right.**
- I re-solved 8 frames in OpenDSS. Every value on screen matches to display rounding, on all 379 transformers.
- The price file matches all 720 minutes.
- Money, tier counts, minutes, drivers, relief and fault figures all reproduce exactly.

**The defects are elsewhere:**
- **P2's naive useful-capacity headline.** The app's own OpenDSS referee refutes it, but the screen still shows it.
- **P2 panels computed only for `core-d26-g0`.** They show that combo's values on every other combo.
- **One wrong beat number** (back-feed).
- **The money band's framing.**
- **A handful of labels.**

Count: **1 HIGH, 6 MEDIUM, 15 LOW.**
