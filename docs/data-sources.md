# Data sources, licences and labels

Every number on screen carries one of four labels. This page lists where each input comes from, its licence, and how it is labelled. The app replays committed files only: **no live data calls at view time**.

| Label | Covers |
|---|---|
| **REAL** | ERCOT prices; SMART-DS topology, kVA and modelled transformer ratings (a REAL dataset of a **synthetic** feeder); OSM footprints; sourced Base and programme facts |
| **SIM** | our simulation's output (OpenDSS AC power flow, the per-transformer surrogate, the month model) |
| **DERIVED** | arithmetic on REAL or SIM: dollars, interpolated 1-minute load, the discharge plan, the D-26 onset, cliffs |
| **ASSUMPTION** | a named constant we chose: margins, the fuse rule, SoC at 16:00, growth, the 2018-load / 2026-price pairing, the fleet placement, the price zone |

Four-home's `SOURCED` maps to REAL; `UNVERIFIED` maps to ASSUMPTION with "unverified" kept in the cite. Every constant is registered once in `sim/constants.py` with its label and cite, and exported into every JSON file's envelope. The audited numbers for the video are in [`docs/NUMBERS.md`](../docs/NUMBERS.md); the audits behind this page are `previous-work/simulators/rz/judges/DATA-TRUTH-inputs.md` and `DATA-TRUTH-outputs.md` (26 Sep 2026).

## The pairing, disclosed

2018 SMART-DS load is paired with 2026 ERCOT prices **by calendar date** (ASSUMPTION, `LOAD_PAIRING`). That mixes weekdays on some evenings:

| 2026 evening | 2026 weekday | 2018 load day used | 2018 weekday |
|---|---|---|---|
| 22 Jul (ERCOT record demand) | Wed | 22 Jul 2018 | **Sun** |
| 14 Aug | Fri | 14 Aug 2018 | Tue |
| 23 Aug (the demo evening) | **Sun** | 23 Aug 2018 | Thu |
| 26 Aug | Wed | 26 Aug 2018 | **Sun** |

**The clock.** Every SMART-DS profile has 35,040 values (365 × 96), so the series has no daylight-saving shifts (DERIVED). If it is in standard time, every August load sits **one hour early** against the CDT prices, including A's 16:45 peak and the 16:00 transformer-peak mode (`PROFILE_INDEX_RULE`, unverified). A +1 h sensitivity run has not been done.

## What we computed (SIM / DERIVED)

- **OpenDSS** (OpenDSSDirect.py 0.9.4) solves the feeder every minute of every evening scenario and refereed the P2 shortlist and the capacity builds. Batteries run at **unity power factor** (ASSUMPTION); the prototype's batteries drew reactive power at OpenDSS's default 0.88 power factor, which overstated battery loading. OpenDSS is the referee, never the controller.
- **The surrogate** (P2 screening) sums home P and Q per transformer with losses from `Transformers.dss`, calibrated against held-out OpenDSS frames; its measured error is in `data/profiles/SOURCE.md` and on the P2 referee badge. A surrogate-only number is labelled **screening**.
- **Tiers** (`sim/tiers.py`, computed once, never in the browser): over nameplate above 100% (amber, not a violation); normal rating exceeded above 110% for 30 minutes or more (the headline violation); emergency above 150%. The protection rule (fuse opens above 200% for 10 minutes or above 300% for 60 seconds) is the team's round-1 **ASSUMPTION**, shown with its margin and never tuned. It is a knife edge on 23 Aug: naive A peaks at 201.2% (SIM) and stays above 200% for 9 minutes (SIM), one short of the rule, so it does not operate; on 14 Aug it operates on 2 transformers (SIM, ASSUMPTION rule).
- **Useful capacity** (Learnings Q1, `ui/data/p2/index.json` `usefulCapacity`): how many batteries fit from an empty feeder before a battery-caused normal-tier transformer event or the feeder head over 370 A. The answer is OpenDSS's: naive holds at **100** and fails at 101 (`naiveOpenDSS`; the per-phase quick estimate said 94, and the screening count of 383 is refuted by OpenDSS); feeder-aware fits **1,007** with 0 battery-caused events. **Voltage is not in this harm test**: in the 1,007-battery build one home dips just under the 0.95 pu floor (0.9498 pu, SIM).
- **Prices:** the D-26 charge onset, the discharge plan (perfect foresight, ASSUMPTION: Base's optimizer is not public) and the price cliffs (a fall of at least half within one 15-minute interval from $60 or more) are DERIVED in `sim/prices.py`.
