# Demo script: the five-minute video

**Status: round 2** (26 Sep 2026). Every beat below exists in `ui/data/beats.json` with the same id, link and clock (`ui/test/p2.test.js` checks it). Every beat now has a one-sentence **headline** as well as its caption; on P1 the headline says what the on-screen story line says, so the video and the replay tell the same story.

RZ records by clicking; no terminal on screen. Each beat is **one deep link**. Its headline and caption are built from the committed JSON at view time, and every number in them carries its tag (R, S, D or A), so **read them as written**: if the data refuted an expectation, the caption already says what was measured instead. Never say a number the screen does not show.

## Before you record

1. `scripts/serve.sh`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/ui/?view=more at 1920 x 1080.
2. The More tab lists every beat with its headline (open "Caption" for the full text). Click through once:
   - **no yellow FIXTURE banner** on any beat;
   - **no "(not built yet)"** in anything you plan to read (a clause whose data is not built is left out, never guessed);
   - the P1 beats play in 3D (if the 3D area is blank, add `&nowebgl=1` for the 2D fallback).
3. Each beat's caption bar has **next beat ›**: that is the whole click path. On P1 the bar shows the headline; "Read the full caption" opens the rest.
4. **Playback.** P1 opens paused. The default speed is **0.25×** (2.5 simulated minutes a second); use **0.1×** to linger and **‹ 1 min ›** to step. "Hold" pauses 1.5 s at each story moment and continues: leave it on for narrated takes. **CC** (or `&cap=0`) hides the story line for a clean take.
5. **Optional cold open (before 0:00):** a bare `?view=p1` shows the intro card over the naive street at 21:55: "96 home batteries. One cheap-power signal. What happens to this street's transformers?" Click **▶ Watch it happen** and let the chain light up (price drops → every battery charges at once → transformers overload). Beat links never show the card.

## How to read the screen while you talk

- **Story chain** (top-left over the 3D): three steps per scenario. A step lights when it happens and stays ghosted before; the line under it is the story line, "HH:MM + one sentence".
- **Meters** above the named transformers: the box is the rating, load that sticks out of the lid is too much; a jagged top is past the emergency rating. The halo marks any transformer over its rating.
- **Batteries**: a white cabinet beside the house with a battery icon. Fill = charge; a bolt = charging (teal), an out-arrow = sending power out (violet), no glyph = waiting, crossed signal = silent.
- **Pad-mount** transformers are green boxes on the ground; **pole-mount** ones are grey cans on a pole. Their colour never changes with load; the meter and halo do.
- **Hover** anything (a house, a cabinet, a transformer, a tag) for what it is and what is happening to it now.
- **The day chip** (P1, top of the panel) switches between real ERCOT evenings: 22 Jul (Texas's record demand), 14 Aug (a quiet night), 23 Aug (the evening we know best), 26 Aug (August's priciest evening). "+ Failures" is scripted for 23 Aug only.

## Words to use, and to avoid

- Say **"over nameplate (amber; not a failure)"** for A's afternoon peak. Never "overheats".
- Say **"no service transformer passes its limit"**, never "nothing passes its limit".
- Say **"the naive branch (an assumption)"**, never "how Base charges today".
- Say **"protection may operate (ASSUMPTION rule)"** for dark homes.
- Say **"local relief is an opportunity, unpriced"**, never revenue.
- Say **"gross energy value, not Base's profit"** for every dollar figure.
- Say **"a grid-scale storage revenue benchmark that includes arbitrage"** for the $/kW-month band, never "capacity value" or "capacity payment" (ERCOT pays no capacity).
- A number with the dotted **≈** tag comes from the per-transformer surrogate (screening), not OpenDSS: say "screening". Where the card shows a **✓ OpenDSS** number, read that one.
- The feeder is an **Oncor-suburb stand-in settled at LZ_NORTH (placeholder)**; its real buses sit in Pedernales Electric Cooperative territory. 2018 SMART-DS load is paired with 2026 prices by calendar date (ASSUMPTION).
- **The next-battery score is not a Base product.** Base schedules installs by demand; we add the grid lens.
