# Demo script: the five-minute video (four story pages)

**Status: submission cut** (26 Sep 2026). The video walks the four story pages of the root app, **Configure → Run → Results → Learnings** (with the honest Running screen between Configure and Run). Every beat below exists in `ui/data/beats.json` with the same id, link and clock. Every number in the "Say" column carries its label (REAL, SIM, DERIVED, ASSUMPTION; **screening** where OpenDSS did not check it) and comes from a committed data file: the audited table, with the file and field for each number, is [`presentation/NUMBERS.md`](../presentation/NUMBERS.md). **Read numbers as the page shows them, never round a claim up, and never say a number that is neither on the page nor in NUMBERS.md.**

## Before you record

1. `scripts/serve.sh`, then open the Configure page at 1920 × 1080: http://127.0.0.1:8765/ui/index.html
2. Click through every beat link below once, in a fresh clone:
   - every number on the page carries a tag, and **no page shows a placeholder or "not built"** in anything you plan to read;
   - the Run page plays in 3D (if the 3D area is blank, reload; the 2D fallback is acceptable for a take);
   - the **Running** screen shows the files loading and the engine's measured cost for that run, never a fake progress bar.
3. **Playback.** A Run deep link opens paused at its minute; arriving through **Start the sim →** and Running, it plays from 16:00. The default speed is **0.25×** (2.5 simulated minutes a second); `&speed=0.1` lingers, `&speed=1` skims. `&k=<step>` sets the minute (the trailing `&beat=<id>` only names the beat; the story pages ignore it): k counts minutes after 16:00 (k=360 is 22:00, k=390 is 22:30).
4. The old tab app is kept as the **Engine explorer** (`ui/explore.html`, footer link). It is not in the video.

## How to read the screen while you talk

- **Configure:** evening × policy × failure, plus the fleet levers (size, class, reserve, start charge, home-load growth), each a real engine run on 23 Aug, one lever away from the default. Anything not run is disabled with its reason. Presets: "The demo evening", "Record demand", "Priciest evening", "Pieces fail", "Controller crash", "Hidden attacker".
- **Run:** the 3D street, the rule-generated story line ("22:00 T-150 (A) passes 150%: emergency rating (197.4%)"), the lanes underneath (worst transformer %, street A–D and T-240, price, fleet charge, fleet power), and the "Right now" card with any failure in red.
- **Results:** the verdict and four tiles (worst transformer, normal-rating events, lowest home voltage, fleet charged by 04:00), each with every other committed run beneath it; voltage by bus from the substation outwards; reactive power. Only the `covert` scenario shows ERCOT context.
- **Learnings:** four questions. Q1 compares naive and feeder-aware capacity (OpenDSS); Q2 is one transformer, 0 to 50 batteries; Q3 is which transformers are worth upgrading, each one priced (the list holds at +20% and +50% home load); Q4 is where the next battery helps most.
- **Tier colours:** tier 0 is sage, never bright green: **green never means safe**.

## Words to use, and to avoid

- **Naive:** "our assumption of one number, no feeder check". Never "how Base charges today".
- **The feeder:** "an Oncor-suburb stand-in on NREL's synthetic feeder" (REAL dataset, synthetic feeder); 1,010 **customers** (971 homes, 39 small businesses). Its coordinates really fall in a co-op's territory.
- **The fleet:** "a deliberate stress placement": 24 clustered on purpose plus 72 random, 9.5% of customers.
- **Every no-violation claim** ends "**because of batteries**": "no transformer passes its limit because of batteries". T-240 goes over nameplate on home load alone.
- **Over nameplate** (above 100%) is amber, **not a failure**; a normal-rating violation is above 110% for 30 minutes or more; emergency is above 150%. Never "overheats".
- **Money:** "the fleet's **gross energy value, not Base's profit**". The per-Core year ($284.68, DERIVED) assumes **perfect foresight**: say so.
- **Houston:** "Base's **set point** went from 0 to −45.8 MW in 15 minutes"; the fleet delivered −44.7 MW. Never "−15.9 → −45.8", never "ERCOT's base point".
- **Scale:** 40 kW is 160% of A's 25 kVA can, but 1.5% of **one conductor of the head cable** (2,663.8 kVA), never "of this feeder".
- **Screening:** any number from the per-transformer surrogate, not re-checked by OpenDSS, is "screening". Where OpenDSS checked it, read the OpenDSS number.
- **Protection:** "may operate (ASSUMPTION rule)". Say the 9-of-10-minute margin on 23 Aug. In the August month, "protection may operate in 2 naive candidate placements" is **screening** (surrogate peaks, not OpenDSS-checked); the 3 existing-fleet transformers (A, C, B) are OpenDSS-confirmed.
- **Ranks and counts carry their denominator:** "rank 345 of 353" under naive for feeder-aware's #1; "802 of 911 places decided by id"; "109 of 379 transformers". August's 673 vs 2 hours above nameplate (naive vs feeder-aware) is **screening**.
- **Attacker:** "a **fictional** attacker". Never name a real company.
- **Frequency:** a **3–17 mHz band** for a 1,000-battery hijack. Never "3–5 mHz", never one value.
- **Local relief** is an opportunity, unpriced, never revenue. The $3.12/kW-month figure is a grid-scale storage **revenue** benchmark that includes arbitrage, never "capacity value".
- **The clock caveat:** 2018 load is paired with 2026 prices by calendar date, and the SMART-DS clock may sit one hour early (unverified). If asked about peak hours, say so: transformers most often peak at 16:00 (109 of 379, SIM), prices most often at 18:00 (10 of 31 days, REAL).
- **The next-battery score is not a Base product.** Base schedules installs by demand; we add the grid lens.
